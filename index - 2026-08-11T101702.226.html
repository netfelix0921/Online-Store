From thread to doorstep

Four stages, none of them fast

Stage 01 · Dye

Indigo and madder

Cotton is dipped in tayum indigo up to twelve times. The colour deepens with each pass and never quite matches the last batch.

Stage 02 · Warp

Counting the threads

Around 1,200 warp threads are strung by hand across the loom. This alone takes two days and cannot be undone halfway.

Stage 03 · Weave

Foot on the pedal

A weaver moves through roughly 20cm a day. A double-width blanket is three weeks of mornings.

Stage 04 · Finish

Washed and signed

Each piece is washed twice, hemmed, then signed on the label by the person who made it.
